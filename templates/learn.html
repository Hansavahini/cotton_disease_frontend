Cotton Disease Detection and Recommendations

Organic & Chemical Solutions

Our system provides accurate disease detection for cotton crops and offers both organic and chemical solutions tailored to specific diseases like Powdery Mildew and Bacterial Blight.

We recommend:

  • Mancozeb: 50 gm/pump
  • Carbendazim: 50 gm/pump
These recommendations are based on scientific research and verified by authorized agricultural organizations.

Why Trust Us?

Our recommendations align with trusted resources and research by government-authorized bodies. For more information, visit:
